## Conclusion

A **general web search** is not available to a robots-compliant, keyless, free runner from this
environment: every engine either disallows `/search` for generic agents in robots.txt, or answers
with a CAPTCHA/anti-bot challenge, or needs a paid key. That is a finding the report must state, and
it is the reason the ticket's search-operator examples are expressed as **URL patterns and index
enumeration** instead.

The round therefore uses two discovery channels:

1. **Enumeration** (fully repeatable by the committed script, robots-checked at every hop): the
   repo's existing `html-listing-pdf` / `sitemap-keyword` strategies plus a new `directory-index`
   strategy for Apache autoindexes, applied to a wide host set.
2. **Direct known document URLs** (the taxonomy's `direct-known-document` type): URLs whose location
   was established in the operator's discovery pass, re-fetched by the harvester from their origin
   host under the robots, rate, hash and licence gates. Their provenance is recorded per candidate
   as `discovery_channel: "operator-discovery-pass"`.

Channel 2 is **not** reproducible by the committed script and the report says so. The operator action
that would close the gap is a free `EXA_API_KEY` (agent-reach's documented search backend), which
enables the repo's existing `agent-reach-search` provider with no new code.
