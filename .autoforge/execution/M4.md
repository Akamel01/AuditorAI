# M4 — round-2 report generator and its guard

**Files**: `scripts/harvest-track-a-round2-report.ts` (new),
`tests/discovery/harvest-track-a-round2-report.test.ts` (new).

## The generator

`npx tsx scripts/harvest-track-a-round2-report.ts` regenerates
`docs/RSA-Documents/track-a-round2-harvest-report.md` **byte-identically** from committed inputs
only — no network, no model call, no timestamp. It imports the round-1 rule functions
(`mergeRows`, `parseLicenceRegister`, `CANADIAN_CELLS`, `MANIFEST_STAGE_TO_CELL`) rather than
re-implementing them, so the two rounds are measured by the same rules.

Sections: §0 what round 2 did; §1 AC1 coverage before/after with the priority cells and the
near-miss table; §2 AC2 the corpus class tables over |A'| and |B'| plus the round-2 funnel over the
candidates examined; §3 AC3 the source-yield table; §4 AC4 the gaps with a per-family Track B
(ATIP) ask; §5 provenance and limits.

## The guard (13 tests)

- the committed report equals a fresh regeneration, byte for byte;
- the report carries no timestamp;
- the four acceptance criteria are present as report sections;
- no T0/T1 row is ever counted as coverage;
- a duplicate copy never carries a count;
- the "before" column equals the round-1 coverage numbers;
- every round-1 row survives unchanged;
- the funnel rows sum to the candidate log's row count;
- every attempted source type has a yield row;
- the byte accounting arithmetic holds and stays inside the cap;
- every source the round attempted has a licence-register row;
- an ingested row is in the manifest and is not a hash skip;
- the round-1 report file still says "Track A" and mentions no round-2 artefact.

## The honest result

0 new coverage candidates. The coverage table is unchanged (1/13, 0/41, 1/41) and the report says
so in fixed words. The round's value is the precision, yield and gaps evidence, and the near-miss
table: two Canadian T3 documents (the OC Transpo `Transitway Station Design Safety Audit` and the
UNB conference paper) are ingested but name no stage the classifier recognises, so they land in
`UNSTAGED` / `PRE_OPENING` and never reach a cell. **The stage vocabulary, not the search, is the
bottleneck.**
