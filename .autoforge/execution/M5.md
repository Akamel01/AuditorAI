  aborted. Two fixes landed: the per-document byte cap is now checked *before* the write as well as
  after the download (it previously allowed a document larger than 100 MiB through), and a failed
  write is now skipped with reason `write error` instead of aborting the round.
- **The byte cap was not the cause.** The corpus shelf holds 16 MB; the disk pressure came from
  outside the worktree.
