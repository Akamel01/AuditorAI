# AuditorAI Reference Manifest — United Arab Emirates / Abu Dhabi (AE)

- **Jurisdiction:** AE
- **Updated:** 2026-08-23
- **Collection policy:** public access only; no portal registration, paywall circumvention, or unofficial rehost downloads (Scribd copies exist for several UAE RSA manuals and were deliberately NOT used).
- **Access note:** `jawdah.qcc.abudhabi.ae` (Abu Dhabi QCC standards host) times out / refuses connections from outside the UAE (curl timeout after 60 s; webfetch transport error on 2026-08-23). Official documents were therefore retrieved from Internet Archive Wayback Machine captures of the exact canonical URLs. This is public access; capture dates are recorded per row.
- **Identity caveat:** no PDF text-extraction library was available in the collection environment; document identity rests on (a) exact URL provenance, (b) PDF internal metadata where present, (c) third-party citation of the URL to the named edition. Flagged per row.

## Method & surprises

- The single most valuable discovery: **QCC publishes its entire unified-standards library at predictable URLs** (`jawdah.qcc.abudhabi.ae/en/Registration/QCCServices/Services/STD/ISGL/ISGL-LIST/<CODE>.pdf`, codes TR-/DP-/PR-/WA-/EN-/ROW-) — but the host appears geo-fenced. **Wayback holds broad CDX coverage of that library (~30 docs observed, incl. TR-503/506/509/510/511/516/517/522/526/530/538/542/544)** — a ready-made retrieval path for future AE reference work without touching the geo-blocked origin.
- TR-540's auditor-accreditation annex (CV template, experience rules) is inside the fetched manual — relevant to AuditorAI's "qualified auditor" role modeling.
- Expect a TR-540 revision integrating the Safe Systems Approach (announced May 2025, per CIHT bulletin row 3); schedule a re-check of the live URL.
- Attempt budget used: well under cap — 2 failed direct hits on the geo-fenced origin (recorded as gate evidence), 1 archive metadata query, 4 successful fetches, plus negative probes (rta.ae/dot.abudhabi.ae Wayback CDX empty for RSA docs; GTKP page JS-gated; MoEI nothing public).
