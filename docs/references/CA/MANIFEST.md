## Notes for corpus use

- The **Alberta 2004** and **BC 2004** documents both contain TAC-vs-province **stage equivalency tables** — direct support for CONTEXT.md's Native-Stage vs Canonical-Stage distinction and EV-CA-006 (Alberta Stage 3 = detailed design ≠ UK Stage 3).
- The **MTQ 2012** guide provides French-native vocabulary ("audit de sécurité routière", stages avant-projet préliminaire / avant-projet détaillé / après ouverture) for the Quebec framework.
- **Good Roads 2023** is the most recent Canadian-language English RSA guideline in this set and reflects Austroads-influenced modern practice; treat as industry-association guidance rather than government regulation.
