# UAE Road Safety Audit Practice — Research Artifact (Ticket R5)

> **Jurisdiction warning.** Every claim below describes *Emirate-specific* rules. Abu Dhabi's
> Stage 0, its Stage 1/2 combination rule, its exemption-certificate regime, shelf-life rules,
> and Abu Dhabi Police's role are **not** universal RSA semantics and must be encoded in the
> MVP as explicit UAE/Abu Dhabi behavior, never folded into a generic stage model. See the
> UK pack (`uk-rsa-research.md`), which proves the UK national standard has **no** Stage 0 at
> all, so the app cannot present Stage 0 as a neutral default.

Notes:

- Within Abu Dhabi the manual names several coexisting road authorities — the Department of
  Transport, and municipalities for Abu Dhabi city, Al Ain and the Al Dhafra Region — each acting
  as "relevant road authority" for schemes on their networks [EV-AE-001]. Abu Dhabi Police is a
  shared stakeholder in the audit process, not merely an observer [EV-AE-019].
- The manual's own scope statement covers only the **Emirate of Abu Dhabi Road Network**
  [EV-AE-001]. Its development history cites Dubai practice as an *input*, nothing more:
  "best international practice … in Europe, Australia, New Zealand and the Middle East (Dubai)"
  [EV-AE-002]. Nothing in the manual purports to bind other Emirates.
- QCC acts as the standardisation body for Abu Dhabi road guidelines generally (it also issued
  the Road Speed Management Guidelines in August 2022 and updated the Abu Dhabi Guidelines for
  Road Engineering Design in July 2022), which supports treating TR-540 as the emirate-wide
  normative reference rather than a single-municipality procedure [EV-AE-022].
- Dubai practice is demonstrably active — RTA audited ~1,000 km of existing roads in 2019 —
  but the governing Dubai manual itself remained inaccessible to this research [EV-AE-028][EV-AE-025].

## Stage model

Abu Dhabi native stages (all definitions from the 2018 DMT manual):

| Stage | Native name | Timing / trigger | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stage 0** | Feasibility / Conceptual Design RSA | During feasibility or conceptual design, per option, for large-scale developments, route realignments, new major inter-urban links, major transport developments; accompanies Master Plan/TIS when required; considered at concept stage of **all private developments** (exemptable) | [EV-AE-004][EV-AE-005] |
| **Stage 1** | Preliminary Design RSA | Near to but prior to completion of preliminary design; before commitment to construct or land purchase | [EV-AE-006] |
| **Stage 1/2** | Combined Preliminary + Detailed Design RSA (**single report**) | Permitted where the scheme is small enough that no separate feasibility or preliminary audit was warranted and the project proceeds directly to detailed design | [EV-AE-012] |
| **Stage 2** | Detailed Design RSA | At completion of detailed design, **before tender or construction**; DB/DBO contracts provide interim RSAs of evolving final designs | [EV-AE-007] |
| **Stage 3a** | Pre-Opening RSA | At substantial completion, preferably before opening to traffic | [EV-AE-008] |
| **Stage 3b** | Opening RSA | Where pre-opening audit was not feasible: phased or complete, **within two weeks of opening but before handover** | [EV-AE-008] |
| **Stage 4a** | Post-opening monitoring | 12-month anniversary; crash-data driven | [EV-AE-009] |
| **Stage 4b** | Post-opening monitoring | 36-month anniversary; crash-data driven | [EV-AE-009] |
| Interim | Interim RSA (any stage) | Client-discretionary; never a substitute for formal Stages 1–3 | [EV-AE-021] |
| WZTTM | Work-Zone Temporary Traffic Management audit | Large/exceptionally complex temporary traffic management (≥6 months network impact); report in Stage 3 format | [EV-AE-027] |

### Explicit jurisdiction-specific behavior (must be encoded, never universalised)

1. **Stage 0 is native to Abu Dhabi.** It is a defined, numbered audit stage with mandatory
   triggers (Master Plan/TIS attachment; consideration for all private developments) and its own
   report obligations [EV-AE-004][EV-AE-005]. The UK standard contains no equivalent stage
   (see `uk-rsa-research.md`), and US federal guidance uses named phases instead of numbered
   stages (see `usa-rsa-research.md`). Any MVP screen showing "Stage 0" must label it as
   Abu Dhabi semantics.
2. **Full-range mandate for major schemes.** For new major transport/road projects, Stages
   0, 1, 2, 3 and 4(a/b) are *all mandatory* [EV-AE-010].
3. **Permitted Stage 1/2 combination for smaller schemes.** Where no Stage 0/Stage 1 was
   warranted and the project goes straight to detailed design, Stages 1 and 2 collapse into one
   "Stage 1/2" report; the road authority is consulted on the stage set actually required
   [EV-AE-012].
4. **Stage 0 and/or Stage 1 exemptions.** Either or both early stages may be waived for scale
   reasons, but only with early, documented road-authority approval recorded in the design file
   [EV-AE-011], formalised through the Appendix C Exemption Note/Certificate with six enumerated
   dispositions (deferred; Stage 1/2-at-detail-design for minor works; negligible-effect exempt;
   like-for-like maintenance exempt; furniture-refurbishment exempt; other) [EV-AE-014].
5. **Downgraded outputs for minor schemes.** The Appendix A matrix allows outcomes as light as
   "RSA Feedback Form Only" for minor private developments and minor rehabilitation works, and
   outright EXEMPT status for like-for-like maintenance (with encouragement to fix observed
   safety issues opportunistically) [EV-AE-013].
6. **Shelf-life and re-audit triggers are codified** (void after 6 months incomplete; re-audit
   after material change or 2 years; 5-year tolerance for Stage 0; authority extension capped at
   4 years) — a rule set not seen in UK/US national guidance [EV-AE-017].

### Dubai / other Emirates differences

- Dubai maintains its own manual distinct from TR-540 [EV-AE-025], and RTA runs network-scale
  audits of *existing* roads [EV-AE-028], but whether Dubai uses a Stage 0 or permits Stage 1/2
  combinations is **Unknown — unverifiable without the document**.
- No other Emirate publishes an RSA manual that this research could locate. Do **not** extend
  Abu Dhabi rules to them.

## Inputs per stage

The Audit Brief checklist (clause 5.10.2) governs inputs at every stage; stage-specific deltas
from the manual:

- **Stage 0:** concept/master-plan options, route alignments, development or transport plan,
  tie-ins to the existing network, junction formats and land-take constraints; the audit
  attaches to Master Plan and Traffic Impact Study submissions when triggered [EV-AE-005].
- **Stage 1:** general-detail drawings (route alignment, vertical profile, typical
  cross-sections), junction/interchange layouts at larger scale; the brief should include
  previous Stage 0 report and responses; unresolved Stage 0 issues are reiterated [EV-AE-006].
- **Stage 1/2 (combined):** detailed-design drawing set covering horizontal/vertical alignment,
  cross-sections (lanes, shoulders, drainage, medians, sidewalks, clear zone, roadside
  barriers/hazards), intersection layouts with control devices/signage/markings, interchange
  details (advance/exit signage, accel/decel lanes, gore treatment) — i.e. the union of Stage 1
  and Stage 2 inputs in a single brief [EV-AE-007][EV-AE-012].
- **Stage 2:** the full detailed-design set above plus departures/relaxations from standard,
  traffic signal phasing diagrams, and all prior audit reports/response forms [EV-AE-007].
- **Stage 3:** substantially complete works; tie-ins; the brief includes confirmation of
  construction dates and all previous approvals; police and highway-authority representatives
  join the site visit [EV-AE-008][EV-AE-019].
- **Stage 4:** 12/36-month personal-injury crash data supplied by Abu Dhabi Police for the scheme
  plus adjacent affected network, confirmation of construction/opening dates, notable events since
  construction, and all previous exception reports [EV-AE-009][EV-AE-019].
- **Common inputs:** audit brief with project description/purpose, A4/A3 markable plans,
  traffic/pedestrian flow data where available, and any other context the client officer deems
  necessary; the team may demand further information if the brief is inadequate [EV-AE-019].

## Outputs per stage

Reports, response obligations, and closure mechanics (uniform machinery across stages, with
police distribution at 3/4):

- **Audit report (every stage):** fixed content — scheme description, stage + team + contributors,
  site-visit record (who, when, weather/conditions), specific problems with background reasoning,
  marked-up A3/A4 location maps and photographs, a signed Audit Team Leader statement, and the
  list of documents/drawings examined; each problem gets its own statement of location, nature and
  likely crash types followed by a recommendation [EV-AE-019][EV-AE-020].
- **Draft comment window:** the highway authority receives the draft per stage and must comment
  within **7 working days** [EV-AE-019].
- **Distribution:** finished report + marked-up drawings go simultaneously to client,
  designer/contractor and roads authority; Stage 3/4 outputs additionally copied to Abu Dhabi
  Police [EV-AE-019].
- **Designer/Client response:** every problem answered formally on the feedback form / exception
  report; rejecting a recommendation requires the reasons it cannot be implemented, alternatives
  considered, and the difficulties involved; copies go simultaneously to the Audit Team Leader and
  the roads authority [EV-AE-019].
- **Auditor verification loop:** the auditor reviews each response to confirm the hazard and risk
  were understood and either accepted or reasonably countered, then recirculates the completed
  form [EV-AE-019].
- **Exemption report closure:** the roads authority's safety section finalises and signs the
  feedback/exemption report, optionally convening a meeting first; this signature *completes* the
  RSA process, and until it exists the design stage is officially **incomplete** [EV-AE-019].
- **Rejection rights:** Abu Dhabi Police and the roads authority may reject any audit or exception
  report where road safety would be compromised [EV-AE-019].
- **Exemption certificate:** where a stage is waived, the Appendix C certificate (six categories)
  is the output artifact filed in the design record [EV-AE-014].
- **WZTTM audit:** report follows the Stage 3 format; during construction authorities may raise a
  Dangerous Defect Notification up to an Order to Cease Works [EV-AE-027].

Operational teeth: ITC will not close a right-of-way works permit without Stage 3 RSA approval
from the respective municipality plus written confirmation that all RSA comments are addressed
[EV-AE-024].

## Team and process requirements

- **Minimum team:** two persons — Audit Team Leader + Audit Team Member; no upper limit;
  Observers normally capped at two; Specialist Advisors (e.g., complex signals) advise without
  vote [EV-AE-015][EV-AE-016].
- **Leader qualification:** 5 years safety engineering / crash analysis / collision investigation
  (reduced to 2 for the Al Ain / Al Dhafra municipal context per the manual's "ATM" allowance);
  a 10-day recognised collision-investigation or road-safety-engineering course; ≥5 RSAs in the
  past 12 months as Leader or Member; 2 days CPD in the past 12 months; demonstrated well-rounded
  highway-design knowledge [EV-AE-015].
- **Member qualification:** 2 years relevant experience; same 10-day course; 5 RSAs in the past 24
  months as Member/Observer; 2 days CPD [EV-AE-015]. Observer: 1 year experience plus CPD and
  willingness to learn [EV-AE-015].
- **Peculiar Abu Dhabi rules:** full-time professional auditors (auditing to the exclusion of
  other crash-investigation/road-safety work) are **unacceptable** [EV-AE-015]; approvals are
  project- and stage-specific, so reuse on past projects confers nothing [EV-AE-016];
  misleading CV claims bar the individual from future road-safety work in the Emirate [EV-AE-001].
- **Independence:** no team member may have had any connection with the design; design teams must
  not influence findings (direct clarification contact is allowed and must be logged in the report
  introduction); neither client nor design organisation may petition the team to change the report
  [EV-AE-016][EV-AE-019].
- **Process skeleton:** client initiates → team approved by roads authority → audit brief prepared
  and assessed → joint daytime (plus night-time at 3/4) site visit → report compiled → draft to
  highway authority (7 working-day window) → issue → formal responses → auditor review → roads
  authority signs exemption report → changes implemented. Guidance timing: allow 2–3 working weeks
  from complete brief to report delivery for typical complexity [EV-AE-017][EV-AE-018][EV-AE-019].
- **Interim audits** may be inserted anywhere at client discretion, with client-written
  authorisation gating all design-team↔audit-team contact [EV-AE-021].

## Risk/severity semantics used in UAE practice

- **Qualitative, crash-type-based.** Findings must state the *type of crashes likely to occur*
  because of the problem; the specimen report vocabulary is side-swipe, rear shunt, overshoot,
  pull-out, and collisions with non-motorised users (NMUs) — severity appears only qualitatively
  (e.g., excessive speed near schools "will increase injury severity") [EV-AE-020].
- **No numeric matrix in the 2018 manual.** There is no likelihood × severity scoring grid;
  prioritisation language is limited to remediation "according to the likely impact on traffic
  safety" [EV-AE-019][EV-AE-020].
- **Recommendations are advisory** — the auditor "cannot give instructions"; designers may reject
  but must justify and propose alternatives, and the roads authority is the final arbiter and may
  override with an alternative measure or none [EV-AE-019][EV-AE-020].
- **"Monitor" is constrained:** permissible only for identified vehicle manoeuvres or crash
  causation factors with the monitoring task specifically allocated (usually maintenance section
  or police) [EV-AE-020].
- **Incoming change:** the TR-540 update will introduce Safe System–based *quantitative* risk
  assessment — a structured matrix of exposure, likelihood and severity benchmarked to human
  injury tolerance (e.g., pedestrian impacts ≥30 km/h flagged as fatal-risk) — plus team-based
  auditor approvals. This is announced/emerging, **not yet** the operative baseline [EV-AE-023].
- Enforcement reality: no legal penalty is known for ignoring recommendations in the UAE; the
  sanction is refusal of scheme approval (and for works permits, ITC's Stage 3 gate) [EV-AE-026]
  [EV-AE-024].

## Conflicts and gaps

1. **Two versions of the Abu Dhabi manual exist publicly.** The DMT-hosted First Edition
   (January 2018) versus QCC's TR-540 listing describing an amendment ("current amendment
   provides more information regarding…"). The TR-540 PDF could not be fetched from this network,
   so the delta between the 2018 text and the amended TR-540 text is **unverified** [EV-AE-001]
   [EV-AE-022].
2. **A Safe Systems rewrite is in flight.** The May 2025 update will change risk semantics
   (quantitative matrix) and approval mechanics (team-based approvals), meaning any implementation
   built strictly to the 2018 text will age; the app needs a version pin and a migration note
   [EV-AE-023].
3. **Appendix A extraction ambiguity.** The scheme-type matrix's tick placement survived PDF
   extraction imperfectly for some rows (notably minor private developments); the *existence* of
   combined/downgraded outcomes is certain [EV-AE-012][EV-AE-013][EV-AE-014], but exact
   column-per-row assignments for edge rows should be confirmed against the rendered PDF table
   before encoding as data.
4. **Dubai is a black box here.** A named Dubai Road Safety Audit Manual exists, yet no official
   copy is retrievable; all Dubai-specific behavior must remain Unknown rather than inferred from
   the Scribd upload [EV-AE-025].
5. **Terminology drift inside the 2018 manual.** The specimen report references an "Emirate of
   Abu Dhabi Road Safety Audit Manual 2016" and legacy bodies (DoT, DUPM, ADM), while the cover
   says First Edition 2018 under DMT — cosmetic inconsistency, but parsers should not treat the
   example appendix as current law [EV-AE-001].
6. **TTM audit coverage gap acknowledged in practice** despite WZTTM rules existing in the manual
   and TR-531 governing temporary traffic management — field compliance lags the paper standard
   [EV-AE-027].
