# EV-AE-004

Abu Dhabi defines a native 'Stage 0 – Feasibility Road Safety Audit': an early-design-stage RSA used to define high-level safety choices of alignment, transport or development plans, to pre-empt countermeasures, and to help select a preferred option where more than one exists; all small private developments should be assessed by the highway authority's road safety audit team at conception, and a Stage 0 audit is required where it will assist in defining design choices.
