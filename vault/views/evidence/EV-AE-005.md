# EV-AE-005

Stage 0 audits shall be undertaken during feasibility/conceptual design on each probable route or option for large-scale developments, route realignments, new major inter-urban links and major transport developments; a Stage 0 RSA must accompany any Master Plan and TIS identified by the road authority's road safety team as requiring one, and a Stage 0 must also be considered at concept stage for all private developments, with exemptions available from the road authority's road safety team.
