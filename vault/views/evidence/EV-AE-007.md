# EV-AE-007

Abu Dhabi Stage 2 (Detailed Design RSA) shall be undertaken at completion of detailed design but before tender or construction commences; tender/contract documents should provide for the contractor to employ a road-authority-approved RSA team, and Design-Build / Design-Build-Operate contracts should engage an approved RSA team for interim RSAs of final designs as they evolve.
