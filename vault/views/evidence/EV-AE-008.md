# EV-AE-008

Abu Dhabi Stage 3 (Construction/Opening RSA) is undertaken at substantial completion preferably before opening to traffic (Pre-Opening Stage 3a); where pre-opening audit is not feasible it can be completed as a phased or complete opening audit (Stage 3b) within two weeks of opening but before handover; the Stage 3 is essentially a site visit attended by all team members in daylight and darkness, and police should be invited to attend.
