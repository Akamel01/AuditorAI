# EV-AE-009

Abu Dhabi Stage 4 comprises data-driven post-opening investigations at the 12-month (Stage 4a) and 36-month (Stage 4b) anniversaries using crash data supplied by Abu Dhabi Police (which, with SAAED, are the sole collision-data recording agencies in Abu Dhabi); the decision to undertake Stage 4 is determined by post-opening crash history, and where no crashes occurred an audit is not required but a note to that effect must be generated by the relevant road authority.
