# EV-AE-011

All road and transport projects in Abu Dhabi must be audited at design and construction stages, but the Feasibility (Stage 0) and/or Preliminary (Stage 1) design stage audits may be exempted where the project is of a scale deemed not to warrant either one or both of those stages, with exemption approval obtained early in the design process from the relevant roads authority and recorded in the design file.
