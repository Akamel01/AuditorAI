# EV-AE-012

Where an Abu Dhabi project is of a scale that no feasibility or preliminary design stage audit was necessary and it progresses directly to detailed design, Audit Stages 1 and 2 can be combined at Stage 2 and referred to as a 'Stage 1/2' single audit report (the manual also states generally that for smaller schemes Stage 1 and Stage 2 can be combined into a single RSA report), with the road authority consulted on which stages shall be undertaken.
