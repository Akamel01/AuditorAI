# EV-AE-013

Appendix A of the Abu Dhabi manual maps scheme types to permitted audit-stage combinations — major new/route-selection projects get the full set (Stages 0, 1, 2, 3, 4); on-line rehabilitations and street-furniture additions skip Stage 0; medium works run Stage 2/3/4 exempted from Stage 1; minor private developments and minor rehabilitations reduce to a combined Stage 1/2 plus Stage 3 or even 'RSA Feedback Form Only'; complex work-zone traffic management gets a WZTTM audit with feedback form only; and like-for-like maintenance is EXEMPT.
