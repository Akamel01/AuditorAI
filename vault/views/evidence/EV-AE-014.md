# EV-AE-014

The Abu Dhabi RSA Exemption Note/Certificate (required where indicated on the Appendix A table) enumerates six disposition categories: deferred to next design stage (Stage 0 and/or Stage 1 only); minor works with minimal design approved for a Stage 1/2 RSA at detailed design; exempt as minor works of negligible effect on the EADRN; exempt as like-for-like replacement maintenance; exempt as refurbishment of existing street furniture; or exempt for another described reason — signed by the designer and approved by the Roads Authority road safety team, with a copy kept in the design file.
