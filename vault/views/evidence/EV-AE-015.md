# EV-AE-015

An Abu Dhabi RSA team shall comprise no less than two persons (Audit Team Leader plus Audit Team Member); the Leader must demonstrate 5 years (2 for Al Ain/Al Dhafra region municipalities per the manual's ATM allowance) of safety engineering/crash analysis/collision investigation, a 10-day recognised collision-investigation or road-safety-engineering course, at least 5 RSAs in the past 12 months as Leader or Member, 2 days CPD in the past 12 months, and well-rounded highway design knowledge; the Member needs 2 years experience, the same 10-day course, 5 RSAs in the past 24 months as Member/Observer and CPD; auditors who audit full time to the exclusion of other road-safety work are unacceptable.
