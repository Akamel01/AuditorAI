# EV-AE-016

In Abu Dhabi the RSA team is appointed only with the approval of the relevant roads authority, which assesses competence, independence and current registration; approvals are project- and stage-specific; an RSA submitted with an unapproved team will be rejected; Audit Team Observers are normally limited to two; Specialist Advisors advise but are not team members; the team must have had no connection with the design being audited; and the same team is not required across all stages.
