# EV-AE-017

Abu Dhabi audits carry explicit shelf-life rules: an audit is void and must be redone if not completed (designer responses reviewed by the auditor and received by the roads authority) within 6 months; completed Stage 1, combined Stage 1/2 and Stage 2 audits must be repeated if the design materially changes or the previous audit is more than 2 years old (Stage 0 may extend to 5 years); and the roads authority may extend shelf-life at its discretion up to a maximum of four years.
