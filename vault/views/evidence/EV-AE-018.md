# EV-AE-018

Every Abu Dhabi audit stage requires at least a daytime site visit by all team members together, and a night-time site visit is additionally mandatory at all Stage 3 and Stage 4 audits and all Road Safety Inspections, with exemptions from day- or night-time visits possible only for very minor works at the discretion of the road authority's road safety section.
