# EV-AE-019

Abu Dhabi output/response obligations: the Stage 0/1/2/3 report follows a fixed content list (scheme description, stage and team identification, site visit record, problems with reasoning, marked-up map/photos, signed team statement, document list, per-problem statement of location/nature/likely crash types, per-problem recommendation); the highway authority gets the draft for comment within 7 working days; the finished report goes simultaneously to client, designer/contractor and roads authority (plus Police at Stages 3/4); the designer/client must respond formally via the feedback/exception form giving reasons and alternatives for any rejected recommendation; the auditor reviews responses; the roads authority finalises and signs the exemption report, which completes the RSA process — a design stage remains 'incomplete' until the exception report is authorised; and Abu Dhabi Police and the roads authority reserve the right to reject any audit or exception report where road safety would be compromised.
