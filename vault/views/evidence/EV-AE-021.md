# EV-AE-021

Interim Road Safety Audits in Abu Dhabi may be applied at any time during preliminary and construction stages at the Client's discretion, but are explicitly not mandatory and not a substitute for the formal Stage 1, 2 and 3 Safety Audits, and Design Teams may not contact Audit Teams without the Client's prior written authorisation.
