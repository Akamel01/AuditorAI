# EV-AE-022

TR-540 is the QCC-published technical reference for the Emirate of Abu Dhabi Road Safety Audit Manual, described by QCC's infrastructure standards list as a compiled set of RSA rules and policies standardising infrastructure stages in the Emirate of Abu Dhabi, serving as the official reference for all road safety audit activities in the Emirate (covering pre-design and design activities, monitoring, and post-construction site inspection), with a current amendment adding detail on auditor training/experience, audit stages and procedure.
