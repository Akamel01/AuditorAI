# EV-AE-024

Abu Dhabi's ITC traffic-detour permit regime operationally enforces the RSA process: authorised parties must act in accordance with approved manuals including the Emirate of Abu Dhabi Road Safety Audit Manual, and closing out a detour permit requires Road Safety Audit (Stage 3) approval from the respective municipality plus a client letter confirming all comments identified in the RSA report are addressed.
