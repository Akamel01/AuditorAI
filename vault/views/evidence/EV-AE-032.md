# EV-AE-032

TR-540 Second Edition makes auditor independence central to the procedures: no member of the Audit Team may have had any connection with the design of the measures being audited, and that independence must be maintained throughout the audit process (direct contact with the Design Team remains permitted, e.g. for clarifying the Audit Brief).
