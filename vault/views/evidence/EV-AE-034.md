# EV-AE-034

At Stage 3 RSA, TR-540 requires the road safety auditor to be aware of issues impacting all road users including the continuity of pedestrian, cycle, mobility-impaired, micromobility (“micro mobility”) and other vulnerable road user accesses and facilities, and directs reference to an Interim Advice Note on Vulnerable Road Users — VRU provisions embedded directly in the audit workflow.
