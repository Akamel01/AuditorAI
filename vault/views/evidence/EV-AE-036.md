# EV-AE-036

Per the TR-540 update co-author speaking at the May 2025 CIHT UAE seminar, under the forthcoming standard risk will be assessed on Safe System principles such that if a pedestrian can be hit at 30 km/h or above it is a problem, and pedestrians, junctions and roads at prevailing high UAE speed limits do not comply with the Safe Systems Approach — concretising the quantitative-risk direction flagged in [EV-AE-023] and predicting audit conflict over speed.
