# EV-CA-007

Alberta Transportation's Road Safety Audit Guidelines Appendix B (Network Continuity Constraints Register) records two conflicting entries for the Highway 401 express-collector corridor: (1) 'Corridor-wide network continuity must be maintained across all three interchange transfer points per TAC CRSAG Section 5.3' and (2) 'Ramp terminal spacing at Interchange 2 falls below minimum 800 m standard; network continuity cannot be guaranteed at this location without geometric revision' — creating an unresolved scope boundary for the Planning Stage RSA.
