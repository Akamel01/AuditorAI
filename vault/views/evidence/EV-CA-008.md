# EV-CA-008

Alberta Transportation sets audit timing triggers: a Stage 1 audit once the desired functional-planning option is selected (with audits of value-engineering proposals); a Stage 2 audit at the 95% stage of the Functional Plan before final submission; a Stage 2 audit at the 95% stage of bridge Design Data Drawings; a Stage 3 audit prior to the milestone 'Modify Design Based on Recommendations from Others' (before 75% completion, equivalent to the 90-95% design stage); pre-opening audits mandatory for new green-field road construction and considered for retrofit projects when substantially complete; and value-engineering proposals at post-tender/pre-construction must also be audited.
