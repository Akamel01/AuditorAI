# EV-CA-010

Alberta Transportation requires the road safety audit team to consist of a minimum of two people, carried out by specialist consultants trained in RSA practice chosen from a pre-qualified list and retained as a sub-consultant of the planning/design consultant; auditors must have completed a recognized safety audit training course of at least 2 days, have at least 5 years experience in relevant road safety, road design or traffic engineering fields, and have undertaken at least one audit per year in the past two years; a Team Leader must additionally have undertaken at least five formal road safety audits in the past three years including at least three at design stages, and for more complex projects should have undertaken at least 10 audits in the past three years including at least five as Team Leader.
