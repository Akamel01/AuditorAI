# EV-CA-011

Alberta Transportation requires the RSA report to follow TAC Canadian Road Safety Audit Guide procedures with a copy made available to the department, a production timeline agreed between project and audit teams ranging from about 5 days for simple reports up to 20 days for complex projects, and a response report from the project design team within a reasonable period offering three possible responses: accept in full and implement changes; conditionally accept with documented modifications; or reject with reasons; and it is the project owner, not the road safety auditor, who decides whether the response is appropriate.
