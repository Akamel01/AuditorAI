# EV-CA-012

Alberta Transportation frames RSA objectives as minimising the frequency and severity of preventable collisions, considering the safety of all road users including vulnerable road users, ensuring collision mitigation measures are fully considered, and avoiding negative safety impacts outside project limits; it states an RSA is not an opportunity to redesign a project or evaluate options, nor a check of adherence to design guidelines, that suggestions must be consistent with the project stage, that the project design team remains ultimately responsible for the design, and that structural integrity is excluded from audit scope.
