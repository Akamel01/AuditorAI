# EV-CA-013

Alberta Transportation describes in-service road safety audits as a sub-set and distinct stage of road safety audit conducted on roads in service and proactive in nature compared to in-service road safety reviews, most beneficial where collision records are poor, but determined that the in-service road safety audit stage would not be undertaken by the department.
