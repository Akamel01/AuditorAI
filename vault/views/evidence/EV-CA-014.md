# EV-CA-014

BC Ministry of Transportation policy requires every Ministry road project meeting the criteria to undergo a safety audit at the appropriate stages, exempts projects valued under $100,000, directs Project Managers to the Decision Guide in Appendix A of the MoT Guideline, permits discretionary audits where not required, requires audits generally in accordance with the TAC Canadian Road Safety Audit Guide, makes the project team responsible for preparing a detailed response report placed in the project file, and requires copies of both the RSA report and response report be forwarded to the RSA Committee representative.
