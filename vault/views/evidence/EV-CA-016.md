# EV-CA-016

The University of New Brunswick Transportation Group's 1999 Canadian-context Road Safety Audit Guidelines identify five traditional audit stages: feasibility (planning), draft (preliminary/layout) design, detailed design, pre-opening, and post-opening (including existing or in-service facilities), with the feasibility audit assessing conceptual design against route location, road design standards and project scope; the preliminary design audit evaluating intersection/interchange layout, horizontal and vertical alignment, cross section and sight distance before land acquisition is finalized; the detailed design audit upon completion of plans typically prior to contract documents; and the pre-opening audit requiring day and night drive-through inspections performed in adverse weather conditions where possible.
