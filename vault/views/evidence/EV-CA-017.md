# EV-CA-017

The UNB guidelines state that the audit report normally takes the form of a written report submitted to the design team and/or client listing safety deficiencies, should not contain recommended remedial measures although exemplary solutions may be identified, and that the design team, which remains responsible for all design decisions, must give the audit team a documented response addressing all safety recommendations; the audit process runs from selecting the audit team, collecting background information, and a commencement meeting, through methodology, documentation and audit report, to a completion meeting and follow-up.
