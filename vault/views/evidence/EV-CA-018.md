# EV-CA-018

The Ontario Road Safety Audit Guideline published by Good Roads (Revision C dated February 15, 2023, prepared by Safe System Solutions) is the first edition of technical guidance establishing road safety audit as a formal process for municipal roads in Ontario, defines a road safety audit as a formal examination of a future road or traffic project, or an existing road or road-related area, in which a team of appropriately qualified persons identifies deficiencies with the potential to cause crashes, states that an RSA is not simply a compliance check against design standards, and draws heavily from Austroads guidance.
