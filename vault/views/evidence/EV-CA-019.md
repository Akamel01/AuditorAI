# EV-CA-019

The Good Roads Ontario guideline identifies five stages at which RSAs are commonly conducted: feasibility (alignment/general location known but few details exist), concept design (basic details including lane configuration and signs/line marking worked out), detailed design (virtually all details finalized and project ready for tender), pre-opening (after construction when works are substantially complete but before opening to the public, with audits occurring shortly after opening known as post-opening audits ideally no more than three months after), and existing conditions (audit of a road already in service).
