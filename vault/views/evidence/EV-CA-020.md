# EV-CA-020

The Good Roads Ontario guideline requires the audit team to comprise a team leader and at least one other member (minimum two people), generally limited to four people for practical reasons, with all members reviewing the design documentation, attending the site and writing the report; it defines three auditor levels (lead auditor, auditor, trainee auditor with no more than two trainees per team), identifies independence from the design team as the fundamental requirement aside from competency, allows specialist advisors and key stakeholders who do not participate in or influence findings, and treats desktop audits without site visits as non-compliant unless justified and documented.
