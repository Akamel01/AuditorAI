# EV-CA-021

The Good Roads Ontario guideline structures the RSA process into commissioning, conducting and completion phases, requiring an audit brief providing all necessary information, provision of background information including known unresolved safety issues, outstanding deficiencies, design parameters and standards adopted with known departures, trade-offs and community input, a commencement meeting, cooperative review of documentation and site inspection by every team member with photographic/video record, and an audit report containing as a minimum title/project introduction, background information including team composition, audit findings including identification of crash types with associated recommendations assessed using a risk assessment framework and severity guidance matrix, and a formal concluding statement signed by the lead auditor.
