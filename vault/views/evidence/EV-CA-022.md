# EV-CA-022

The City of Ottawa 2026 Transportation Impact Assessment Guidelines provide that once a Transportation Impact Assessment is complete, a Road Modification Approval may be followed by Detailed Design and that a Road Safety Audit may be required based on the type of roadway modification being proposed, with the City identifying during the Road Modification Approval review whether an RSA is required.
