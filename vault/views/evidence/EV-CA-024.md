# EV-CA-024

UK road safety audit practice under DMRB GG 119 is normally undertaken at four key stages: Stage 1 at completion of preliminary design, Stage 2 at completion of detailed design (which can be combined with Stage 1), Stage 3 upon completion of construction, and Stage 4 as a monitoring audit one to three years following construction.
