# EV-CA-025

In United States practice, FHWA defines a road safety audit as the formal safety performance examination of an existing or future road or intersection by an independent, multidisciplinary team that qualitatively estimates and reports on potential road safety issues and identifies opportunities for improvement for all road users, notes RSAs can be used in any phase of project development from planning and preliminary engineering through design and construction and on projects of any size, and describes an eight-step process in which the design team and project owner prepare the formal response and incorporate findings.
