# EV-CA-026

Canadian road safety practice as summarized by the Traffic Injury Research Foundation's Action2Zero program holds that road safety audit teams should represent a variety of experience and expertise including roadway design, traffic operations analysis, maintenance, construction, and human factors, and that audit team members should be independent of the design team that developed the original plans and the facility owner to avoid real or perceived conflicts of interest.
