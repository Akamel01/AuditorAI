# EV-CA-029

BC Ministry of Transportation's Road Safety Audit Guidelines state that although some jurisdictions conduct RSA during construction and on in-service roads, at this time the Ministry does not intend to conduct RSA at those stages, confining the BC audit ladder to pre-opening project phases.
