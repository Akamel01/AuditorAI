# EV-CA-030

BC Ministry of Transportation's Road Safety Audit Guidelines enumerate the possible responses to an RSA report, following TAC Canadian Road Safety Audit Guide procedures: accept in full and implement changes to project; conditionally accept with modifications, with the response report documenting the reasons for the modifications; or reject with reasons.
