# EV-CA-037

The UNB guidelines' highway case study (Appendix C) presents the audit report in tabular format for easy exchange between auditors and client, with three columns: the audit team's observations, suggested possible remediation initiatives, and a space for the client response, returned to the auditors once the client had addressed each issue in writing; this worked example pairs observations with suggested countermeasures even though the body text says reports normally should not contain recommended remedial measures [cf. EV-CA-017].
