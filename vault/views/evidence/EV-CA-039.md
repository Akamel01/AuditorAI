# EV-CA-039

The Northeast Anthony Henday Drive / Highway 16 planning-stage RSA (Alberta Transportation, 2009) records that the 130th Avenue (Anthony Henday) and Broadmoor Blvd (Highway 16) service interchanges sit too close to the Highway 16/Anthony Henday system interchange — spacings of 1970 m and 1670 m against a preferred systems–service spacing of 3000 m — placing them within the system interchange's functional operating area with congestion and collision potential.
