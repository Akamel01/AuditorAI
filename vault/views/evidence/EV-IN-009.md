# EV-IN-009

iRAP describes Road Safety Audit as a formal, independent assessment of the safety performance of a road design performed by a team independent of the design team, notes that several national/international RSA guidelines and manuals exist (CAREC, PIARC, AfDB, FHWA, Austroads, CIHT SoRSA), and states the iRAP methodology may be used in support of road safety audits, inspections and impact assessments rather than replacing them.
