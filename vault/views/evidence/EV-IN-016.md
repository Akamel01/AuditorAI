# EV-IN-016

PIARC 2023R40EN Appendix 1 §10.2 "Preliminary Design" audit prompt list requires explicit pedestrian and cyclist provision at every junction ("Is there adequate provision for pedestrians and cyclists?") and dedicated consideration of cyclists and motorcyclists especially at junctions — making uncontrolled shared-use-path crossings at free-flow ramp terminals a preliminary-design audit item, not a post-construction discovery.
