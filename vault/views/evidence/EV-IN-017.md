# EV-IN-017

The CAREC Road Safety Engineering Manual 1 defines six internationally recognized road safety audit stages following the project lifecycle — feasibility, preliminary design, detailed design, road works, pre-opening, and existing-road (road safety inspections) — noting the earlier an audit is carried out, the easier and cheaper effective changes usually are; its first three native stages map directly onto FEASIBILITY_CONCEPT → PRELIMINARY_DESIGN → DETAILED_DESIGN.
