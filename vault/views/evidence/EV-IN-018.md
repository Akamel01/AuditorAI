# EV-IN-018

CAREC stage checklists are semantically loaded for candidate generation — a negative answer (No) to any checklist question means there is a possible safety issue that may need to enter the audit report, while Yes only means examined-and-unlikely-to-be-an-issue; teams are also warned that checklists are detailed but not exhaustive and that "a successful audit is not achieved by just ticking each item on the checklist."
