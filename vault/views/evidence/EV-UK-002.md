# EV-UK-002

GG 119 clause 1.3 requires that where road safety audit is applied to a highway scheme, it shall be undertaken at each of exactly four stages: Stage 1 (completion of preliminary design), Stage 2 (completion of detailed design), Stage 3 (completion of construction) and Stage 4 (post opening monitoring).
