# EV-UK-003

GG 119 defines road safety audit as the review of highway schemes at the completion of preliminary design, completion of detailed design, completion of construction and as a post-opening monitoring exercise, with stages 1-3 communicating problems and recommendations and stage 4 communicating problems and recommendations based on collision data analysis.
