# EV-UK-005

GG 119 defines an "interim road safety audit" as the application of the RSA process to the whole or part of a scheme at any time during design and construction, states it is neither mandatory nor a substitute for the stage 1, 2, 3 and 4 audits, makes the Overseeing Organisation responsible for deciding whether to undertake one, and requires the interim RSA process to follow the requirements of the relevant stage.
