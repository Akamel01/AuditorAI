# EV-UK-008

GG 119 requires Stage 1 RSA at completion of preliminary design (before publication of draft orders), recommends it include road safety matters bearing on land take, licence or easement, notes this is often the last occasion land requirements can change, and allows a Stage 1 audit to be combined with Stage 2 where preliminary design was not undertaken.
