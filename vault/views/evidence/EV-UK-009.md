# EV-UK-009

GG 119 requires Stage 2 RSA at completion of the detailed design stage, requires the team to review the detailed design information in the brief, and mandates that the Stage 2 audit include a review of RSA actions from the Stage 1 response report and reiterate any incomplete Stage 1 problems and recommendations.
