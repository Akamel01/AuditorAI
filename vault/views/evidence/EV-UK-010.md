# EV-UK-010

GG 119 requires Stage 3 RSA when construction is complete, recommends it before the scheme opens, requires it to be carried out within 1 month of opening unless otherwise agreed, requires the team to examine the scheme from the viewpoints of all road users and to visit together in daylight and during hours of darkness, with police and maintaining agent representatives as invitees.
