# EV-UK-011

GG 119 requires the Overseeing Organisation to arrange Stage 4 post-opening monitoring using 12 months of validated post-opening road traffic collision data, requires a Stage 4 report only where collisions were recorded in the vicinity of the scheme over that period, requires any decision not to proceed with Stage 4 reporting to be recorded on the scheme file, and specifies that a RSA response report is not produced for Stage 4.
