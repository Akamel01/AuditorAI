# EV-UK-012

GG 119 requires the RSA team to comprise a team leader and at least one team member who are independent of the highway scheme conception, design, construction and operation, limits observers to two, requires specialist advisors to be approved by and independent of the team and scheme parties, and requires the Overseeing Organisation to approve the proposed team before the audit for the specific scheme and stage.
