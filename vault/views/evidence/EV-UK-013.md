# EV-UK-013

GG 119 sets team competency expectations of 10 days of formal collision data analysis or road safety engineering/road design training, a minimum of 2 days CPD in the last 12 months, 1 year (member) or 4 years (leader) of relevant experience, 5 completed RSAs within 24 months (member) or 12 months (leader), and requires at least one team member on the motorway/trunk road network to hold an RSA certificate of competency acquired per Appendix G under EU Directive 2008/96/EC, with certificates having no finite validity period.
