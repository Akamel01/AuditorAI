# EV-UK-014

GG 119 requires the design organisation to prepare the RSA brief for stages 1-3 and the Overseeing Organisation to approve and issue it (the Overseeing Organisation prepares it for Stage 4 or where no design organisation exists), and requires the RSA team to produce a written RSA report issued directly to the Overseeing Organisation containing scheme description, brief/team approvals, membership, site visit attendance and conditions, marked-up location plan, a signed team statement certifying compliance with GG 119, and a list of information provided.
