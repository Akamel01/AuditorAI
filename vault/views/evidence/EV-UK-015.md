# EV-UK-015

GG 119 requires each RSA report problem to describe the location, nature and type of collisions or road user injuries likely to occur, followed by a proportionate and viable recommendation, prohibits the words "consider" and "must" in recommendations, restricts recommendations to "monitor" to specifically justified cases, confines the audit to road safety matters affecting all road users, and requires strategic decisions (such as route choice and junction type already agreed by the Overseeing Organisation) to be identified in the brief as outside the audit's ability to change.
