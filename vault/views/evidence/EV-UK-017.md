# EV-UK-017

GG 119 Table 5.42 requires site visits by all team members together limited to six people, with daytime visits at Stages 1 and 2, daytime and darkness visits at Stage 3, Stage 4 visits triggered only by specified collision-data findings (higher than expected numbers, increased rate or severity, common trends, or vulnerable road user matters), and permits varying visit timing to observe specific traffic conditions.
