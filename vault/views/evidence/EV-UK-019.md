# EV-UK-019

GG 119 identifies four Overseeing Organisations (National Highways for England, Transport Scotland, the Welsh Government, and the Department for Infrastructure Northern Ireland), states that the term highway scheme includes road schemes in Scotland, and in v2.0.1 names the Transport Scotland Overseeing Organisation specialist as an appropriate person within the Roads Directorate (Revision 2 had named the Head of Standards).
