# EV-UK-021

The Welsh Government applies the DMRB with Wales-specific annexes to trunk road improvement schemes, and its Practice Advice Guidance 115/20 additionally requires active travel duties under the Active Travel (Wales) Act 2013 and a full departure from guidance submission endorsed by the Chief Highway Engineer on the recommendation of an independent Technical Review Panel where active travel guidance is not followed.
