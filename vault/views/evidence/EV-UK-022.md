# EV-UK-022

CIHT's Society of Road Safety Auditors guidance confirms the UK national standard adopts four audit stages (preliminary design, detailed design, completion of construction, post-opening monitoring) and notes that an additional feasibility stage ("Stage F") is used in some other countries rather than being part of the UK national standard, while advising at least one pre-construction and one post-construction audit stage as a minimum for schemes needing audit.
