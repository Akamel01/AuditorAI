# EV-US-003

The FHWA RSA Guide presents an eight-step process: identify the project or existing road to be audited; select the RSA team; conduct a pre-audit meeting to review project information; perform field reviews under various conditions; conduct audit analysis and prepare a report of findings; present audit findings to the Project Owner/Design Team; prepare a formal response; and incorporate findings into the project when appropriate.
