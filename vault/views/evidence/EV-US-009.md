# EV-US-009

Per the Model Road Safety Audit Policy, RSA team members should have no previous involvement with the project or facility being audited, and teams may be drawn from other divisions of the road agency without prior involvement, other road agencies under reciprocal arrangements, the private sector, or organizations contributing specialist expertise such as maintenance, enforcement, or emergency services.
