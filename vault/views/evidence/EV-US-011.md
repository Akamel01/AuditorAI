# EV-US-011

Each RSA includes a start-up meeting with the RSA team, road owner, and design team; a field review by the RSA team; identification of safety issues on the basis of crash data analysis and/or operational, geometric, and human factors issues; suggestions by the team on how safety can be enhanced; a preliminary findings meeting; and a written report submitted to the road owner, who circulates it to stakeholders, with suggested owner comments on the draft limited to factual corrections or feasibility input within about one week and not directed at changing findings unless they relied on incorrect facts.
