# EV-US-015

FHWA RSA case-study guidance describes three macro-phase audit types: pre-construction RSAs covering planning and design, construction RSAs covering work zone traffic management, design changes during construction, and audits completed after construction but before the roadway opens, and post-construction or operational RSAs of existing roads usually conducted to address a demonstrated crash problem, with the eight-step RSA process following FHWA-SA-06-06.
