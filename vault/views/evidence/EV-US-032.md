# EV-US-032

SCDOT interchange design guidance requires explicit pedestrian and bicyclist treatment at interchanges: signing and lane markings to increase awareness, with signing, crosswalks, barriers, over- and underpasses, bridge sidewalks, and other traffic control devices as may be required to manage traffic movements and control pedestrian and bicycle movements.
