## Question

Does a true authoritative **international** road safety audit standard exist? Investigate
primary material from PIARC (World Road Association, e.g. Road Safety Audit Guideline),
ISO (e.g. ISO 39001 road traffic safety management systems), iRAP methodology documents,
WHO / UN road safety machinery. Determine whether the product's "International" option is
backed by a citable framework or must ship as an explicitly qualified international
baseline synthesized from named sources. Do not invent a standard.

## Resolution

**No true international RSA standard exists.** ISO publishes organizational management-system standards (ISO 39001), not project-stage audit methods; the only mandatory regime found is regional (EU Directive 2008/96/EC as amended). The nearest de-facto international instrument is PIARC guidance — *Road Safety Audits: Guidelines for Road Projects* (2023R40EN) plus the PIARC Road Safety Manual — which PIARC itself frames as non-binding. UN/WHO material recommends audits but prescribes no procedure.

Consequence for the product: the "International" jurisdiction option ships as an **explicitly qualified baseline synthesized from named sources** (PIARC spine, iRAP overlay), with wording that never claims compliance-with-an-international-standard.
