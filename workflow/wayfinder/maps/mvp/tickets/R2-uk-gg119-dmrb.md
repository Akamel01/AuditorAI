## Question

What does current authoritative UK practice define for road safety audit stages?
Sources: National Highways *Standards for Highways* / DMRB **GG 119 Road Safety Audit**
(current revision), plus any Scottish/Welsh/NI equivalents of note. Extract:
native stage names and definitions (does a UK "Stage 0" exist at all?), triggers,
required inputs per stage, audit team composition/independence rules, process steps
(interim and completion reports, designer response, exceptions procedure), and required
outputs/report content.

## Resolution

Authoritative source is DMRB **GG 119 v2.0.1, published 2025-04-30** (Standards for Highways; supersedes Rev 2 Jan 2020). Native UK stages are exactly **Stage 1 (completion of preliminary design), Stage 2 (completion of detailed design), Stage 3 (opening), Stage 4 (post-opening)** per clause 1.3 — **no UK Stage 0 exists**; interim RSAs are the only sanctioned earlier audits. Team: leader + ≥1 member, independent of design/construction/operation, certificate of competency required. Outputs: interim + completion audit briefs/report; **response report and decision log with three designer response options** (accept, partially accept/reject with reasons); exceptions decided by the Overseeing Organisation. GG 119 assigns **no severity scores** — risk assessment sits in designer response.
