## Question

What does current authoritative US practice define? Sources: FHWA *Road Safety Audit*
guidelines and RSA program materials (including the FHWA RSA Guide/Promising Practices),
relevant AASHTO/NCHRP material. Extract: whether formal "stages" exist in US guidance and
under what names (planning/feasibility, preliminary design, detailed/final design,
construction, operational/post-opening), the eight RSA process steps if still current,
multidisciplinary team expectations, field-review requirements, report contents, owner
response obligations. Do NOT map US practice onto UK stage numbers.

## Resolution

US federal guidance defines **no numbered audit stages**. FHWA materials attach RSAs to named **project phases** (planning, preliminary design, final design, work zone, pre-opening, existing-road/operational, land-use development) executed via a single **phase-independent eight-step process**; numbered prompt lists (1–7 in FHWA-SA-06-06) are checklist identifiers, not stages. Team: multidisciplinary, independent, field review expected; owner/designer response expected but timelines are program-level (e.g., SC DOT 45 days is a state program example, not federal rule).

Decision input: model USA as `(project_phase × one process)`; never map US phases onto UK stage numbers.
