## Question

What does authoritative Canadian practice define? Sources: Transportation Association of
Canada (TAC) publications including the Canadian Guide to Road Safety Audits /
Canadian Road Safety Audit Guide (CRSAG) editions, CRaSH program material, and useful
provincial guidance (e.g. BC). Extract: Canadian stage terminology and definitions,
per-stage inputs/outputs, team/process requirements, and how Canadian practice differs
from UK and US practice.

## Resolution

National anchor is TAC's **Canadian Road Safety Audit Guide** (PTM-CRSAG-E, 2001-12-19) — recommended guidance; full text paywalled (recorded, not guessed), known via catalogue metadata + verbatim provincial quotations. Provincial practice carries the operative rules: **Alberta Transportation Guidelines (Mar 2004)** with a Stage 1–3 + pre-opening ladder, team-qualification thresholds and report/response rules; **BC MoTI Circular T-02/04 (2004)** making RSA mandatory within MoTI projects (>$100k) generally per TAC CRSAG; **Ontario Good Roads Guideline Rev C 2023** for municipal roads (Austroads-derived stages + risk matrix). Key hazard discovered: stage IDs collide across jurisdictions (Alberta Stage 3 = detailed design ≠ UK Stage 3 = post-construction) → **stage identifiers must be namespaced per jurisdiction**, never bare numbers. In-service reviews are a separate mode (TAC companion guide), not a numbered stage.
