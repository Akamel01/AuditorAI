## Question

What does authoritative UAE practice define? Sources: Abu Dhabi Quality and Conformity
Council (QCC) *Abu Dhabi Road Safety Audit Manual*, Department of Municipalities and
Transport (DMT) material, and Dubai/RTA or other Emirates guidance where findable. Must
capture: Abu Dhabi's Stage 0 as feasibility/conceptual, permitted Stage 1/2 combinations
for smaller schemes, per-stage inputs/outputs, team rules — represented explicitly as
jurisdiction-specific behavior, never universal RSA semantics. Note whether Abu Dhabi
material represents other Emirates.

## Resolution

Primary verified source: ***Emirate of Abu Dhabi Road Safety Audit Manual*, DMT First Edition, January 2018** (standardized as QCC TR-540; TR-540 amended text unreachable — recorded as inaccessible, related claims medium-confidence). Abu Dhabi semantics, all **jurisdiction-specific**: **Stage 0 = feasibility/conceptual audit is native**; a **combined Stage 1/2 single report is explicitly permitted for smaller schemes**; exemption-certificate regime and shelf-life rules exist; Abu Dhabi Police is a process stakeholder; ITC detour permits hard-wire Stage 3 approval. Dubai RTA manual exists but no official copy accessible (contents Unknown); no other Emirate manuals found. The manual's scope covers only the Abu Dhabi road network — it does not bind other Emirates.
